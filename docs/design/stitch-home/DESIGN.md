## Brand & Style

Horizon is an architectural, ultra-precision financial instrument designed for individuals and institutions who demand mathematical clarity over decorative distraction. Drawing direct heritage from Braun-era functionalism (Dieter Rams) and Scandinavian industrial instrumentation (Teenage Engineering), the interface behaves like a calibrated hardware console machined from titanium, glass, and carbon.

### Core Tenets
- **Sculptural Reductivism:** Decoration is treated as visual noise. Elevation, status, and relationship are established purely via micro-hairlines, monospaced coordinates, and structural spatial logic.
- **Instrumental Tactility:** Interfaces emulate physical studio hardware—monolithic plates, etched registers, click-wheel precision switches, and phosphorescent telemetry indicators.
- **Radical Contrast & Rhythm:** High-contrast structural boundaries pair with absolute editorial confidence: wide architectural headlines, rigid tabular columns, and hyper-restrained kinetic micro-hits.

## Colors

The palette operates with clinical discipline: warm architectural titanium alabaster surfaces punctuated by pitch-black monolithic ink, bound together by technical hairline borders, and illuminated by singular phosphorescent indicators.

### Base Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#F4F4F0`):** Warm titanium alabaster; non-glare, architectural plate foundation.
- **Container Raised (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure pristine white surface layer for discrete interactive modules and focal planes.
- **Obsidian Monolith (`#111215`):** Matte blackened-titanium slab specifically reserved for primary banking credentials, luxury status cards, and deep modal contexts.

### Structural Ink & Technical Wireframe
- **Carbon Ink (`#0E0F12`):** Pure monolithic primary foreground for typography, active strokes, and dense structural elements.
- **Wireframe Hairline (`#E5E5E0`):** Rigid mechanical grid boundaries, dividers, and card borders.
- **Technical Slate (`#7A7D85`):** Secondary metadata, sub-labels, axis markings, and inactive states.

### Phosphor Accents & Financial Semantics
- **Acid Citron (`#D4FF00`):** Electric phosphor yellow-green, restricted strictly to live system telemetry dots, active ledger states, and momentary focal anchors. Never used for large surface fills.
- **International Safety Orange (`#FF4800`):** Surgical alert state for high-stakes operational execution (e.g., transfers, security locks, protocol warnings).
- **Inflow / Jade (`#0D8050`):** Deep architectural forest green for settled positive yields and inward settlement.
- **Outflow / Vermilion (`#E02E2E`):** Pure clinical crimson for withdrawals, fee debits, and margin shifts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy operates through three designated disciplines:
1. **Architectural Command (`Space Grotesk`):** Used across major scale indices, titles, and section coordinates. Set tight with negative tracking to convey engineered rigidity.
2. **Editorial Clarity (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Neutral, human-accessible, and highly legible for long-form explanatory clauses, system descriptions, and disclosures.
3. **Financial Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width figures ensure total vertical alignment down decimal indices in ledgers, balance sheets, and real-time transaction streams. All technical metadata (`label-mono-sm` and `label-mono-xs`) must be forced to uppercase with expanded letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an unyielding structural grid influenced by Dieter Rams’ rational coordinate planes. Modules dock against adjacent containers separated only by crisp hairline seams (`#E5E5E0`).

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid matrix. Single-column stacked monoliths. Outer gutter and margins locked to 16px (`1rem`). Card padding strictly `16px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout. 2-up structural card splits. Outer margin fixed to 32px (`2rem`).
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column architectural canvas. Max-width boundary pinned to 1440px with 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Multi-pane telemetry arrays with hairline docked divisions.

### Spacing Rules
- Zero arbitrary gaps. Spacing progresses predictably in multiples of 4px and 8px.
- Use docked modular layouts where cards share bounding wireframes rather than floating disjointedly.

## Elevation & Depth

Horizon completely rejects conventional blurred drop shadows and ambient floatation. Depth is entirely mechanical, achieved via **tactile layering, hairline borders, and chromatic planar stacking**.

### Layering Rules
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `#F4F4F0` raw architectural base.
- **Layer 1 (Machined Plates):** `#FFFFFF` pure cards enclosed by a sharp `1px solid #E5E5E0` boundary. Zero box-shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Overlays & Menus):** Pure white plate with a dual boundary: `1px solid #0E0F12` and a hard, crisp offset shadow: `2px 2px 0px 0px rgba(14, 15, 18, 0.08)`. No blur radius is permitted.
- **Layer 3 (Obsidian Titanium Credentials):** `#111215` solid slab featuring a hairline specular highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` and edge definition `1px solid #24262E`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 0 (Sharp)** geometry. 

All interactive triggers, inputs, cards, notification banners, and status chips are defined with razor-sharp 0px borders (`border-radius: 0px`). This strict orthogonal stance reinforces structural engineering and eliminates consumer app soft-edges.

*Single Exception:* Real-time live status indicator nodes (telemetry pings) utilize a pure circle (`50%`) to communicate continuous pulse and optical signal behavior.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Monolith):** Solid `#0E0F12` fill, `#FFFFFF` text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px Medium. Sharp rectangular edge (`radius: 0px`). Micro-state active: instant inverse flash (`#FFFFFF` background with `#0E0F12` border and text).
- **Secondary (Machined Wire):** Transparent background, `1px solid #0E0F12`, carbon text. Hover reveals a crisp internal fill of `#E5E5E0`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Pure text trigger set in `JetBrains Mono` with trailing `[ -> ]` ASCII bracket indicators.
- **Surgical Execution:** Solid `#FF4800` fill, white text, strictly reserved for immediate asset liquation or irrecoverable cryptographic execution.

### Telemetry Chips & Status Badges
- Ultra-compact rectangular capsules (height: 20px). Sharp 0px corners.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` bounded by `1px solid #E5E5E0`.
- Includes a 6px circular phosphor beacon:
  - *Active / Real-time:* Solid `#D4FF00` with high-contrast `#0E0F12` outline.
  - *Settling:* Solid `#7A7D85`.
  - *Alert:* Solid `#FF4800`.
- Label formatted strictly in `label-mono-xs` uppercase.

### Cards & Machined Modules
- Fabricated from `#FFFFFF` mounted to `#F4F4F0`.
- Bound by `1px solid #E5E5E0`.
- Internal sections separated by 1px horizontal and vertical technical lines.
- Header bars include technical coordinates (e.g., `SEC_04 // PORTFOLIO_VALUATION`) set in `JetBrains Mono` 9px muted slate.

### Monolithic Obsidian Banking Card
- Digital representation of the physical banking instrument.
- Deep obsidian matte surface (`#111215`) with a hairline wireframe border (`#24262E`).
- Holographic micro-chip embedded as a brushed metallic structural cell in upper left.
- Card number and cardholder name rendered in crisp laser-etched `JetBrains Mono` in stark white and muted titanium silver (`#7A7D85`).

### Ledger Lists & Transactions
- Continuous list format connected by `1px solid #E5E5E0` borders; zero separation gaps between sequential rows.
- Left block: Transaction entity in `body-md` bold, timestamp below in `label-mono-sm` slate.
- Right block: Tabular financial metric in `numeric-md`. 
  - Positive deposits carry a leading `+` in crisp Forest Jade (`#0D8050`).
  - Debits carry a leading `—` in Vermilion (`#E02E2E`).

### Input Fields & Parameter Entry
- Structural box with `#FFFFFF` background and `1px solid #E5E5E0` boundary.
- Focused state transforms the border to an authoritative `1px solid #0E0F12`—no outer fuzzy ring.
- Value entry features monospaced tabular numerals with floating uppercase metadata tags docked inside the top-right corner.
