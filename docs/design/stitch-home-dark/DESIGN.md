## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-luxurious, cyber-editorial aesthetic designed for next-generation institutional wealth engines and sovereign capital management. Rejecting conservative private banking cliches (such as muted gold, heritage navy, and generic slate grey), the interface draws inspiration from high-concept horology, optical physics, and dichroic mineral structures like crystalline bismuth. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an uncompromising deep obsidian void, punctuated by high-tension chromatic collisions: razor-sharp prismatic mint colliding with solarized acid lime and iridescent magenta-indigo undertones. The visual language conveys peerless precision, mathematical elegance, and sovereign autonomy. It feels like an avant-garde horological terminal engineered in an anechoic chamber—silent, profound, and radiating optical energy at precise interaction vectors.

## Colors

The palette operates under absolute chromatic control. The foundation is built upon deep abyss obsidian layers (`#06080D`, `#0B0F17`, and `#121824`), enriched with subterranean ultraviolet and cold cosmic blue undertones rather than flat carbon black.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#0DF2C9` - Prismatic Mint):** Pure optical vibrancy. Reserved for primary executive triggers, critical state indicators, positive ledger velocity, and active focus boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#C8FF00` - Solar Lime / Acid Chartreuse):** Used sparingly as an avant-garde counter-accent for hyper-critical alerts, yield breakthroughs, and precision delta highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#7928CA` - Deep Hyper-Prism Violet):** Forms complex optical undertones, dichroic gradient reflections, and deep ambient atmospheric backdrops when blended against obsidian.
- **Accent Coral-Magenta (`#FF2E63`):** Serves as an expressive, high-contrast counterpoint for drawdowns, risk liquidation parameters, and urgent telemetry vectors.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Void Base`: `#040608` (App foundation / ambient black hole)
  - `Obsidian Layer 1`: `#080B12` (Card and module backings)
  - `Obsidian Layer 2`: `#0F1522` (Interactive surfaces, inputs, hover states)
  - `Border / Dichroic Rim`: Linear gradients crossing `rgba(13, 242, 201, 0.2)` into `rgba(200, 255, 0, 0.05)` and `rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.25)`.
  - `Text Primary`: `#F2F5F8` (Crisp cold diamond white)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#8492A6` (Muted titanium slate)
  - `Text Tertiary`: `#414D60` (Deep graphite)

## Typography

The typography pairs the geometric, technical poise of **Space Grotesk** with the surgical precision of **JetBrains Mono**. 

### Typographic Principles
- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Set in Space Grotesk with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to create a commanding, architectural cadence.
- **Financial Balances & Ledger Telemetry:** Strictly rendered in JetBrains Mono using tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). This guarantees columnar stability across high-frequency ticker feeds, yield curves, and fractional balance changes.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata:** Employs uppercase JetBrains Mono with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to mirror technical instrumentation and the laser engravings of bespoke timepieces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural logic of a high-density financial terminal combined with an haute-couture publication. It employs a rigid 12-column dynamic grid system engineered for modular analytics and spatial depth.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Base Grid & Columns:** A 12-column layout on desktop screens (>1280px) scaling down to 8 columns on tablet (768px-1279px) and 4 columns on mobile (<768px).
- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Generous outer margins (`3rem`) on wide screens create an intentional pedestal effect for the content canvas, framing the platform as a self-contained vault. On mobile devices, margins condense to `1.25rem` to prioritize data density.
- **Micro vs Macro Spacing:** Component interiors maintain compact padding (`0.75rem` to `1.25rem`) to ensure low vertical scroll fatigue during intensive portfolio audits, while inter-module gaps (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) preserve visual sovereignty between unrelated data primitives.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional muddy drop shadows. Instead, depth is conveyed through **prismatic luminosity**, **dichroic rim-lighting**, and **deep obsidian stratification**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Tier 0 (The Deep Abyss - `#040608`):** The ground substrate. Infused with faint, wide-radius radial gradients (`1200px` blur, 3% opacity) in Prismatic Indigo and Cyanide Mint that simulate far-field cosmic nebulae.
2. **Tier 1 (Vault Containers - `#080B12`):** Cards and panels receive a razor-thin, 1px perimeter border rendered in a linear gradient (`135deg`, from `rgba(13, 242, 201, 0.24)` transitioning to `rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.12)` down to `transparent`).
3. **Tier 2 (Elevated Command Modals & Popovers - `#0E1420`):** Elevated modules feature backdrop filtration (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(160%)`) and an ambient chromatic edge halo: `0 0 0 1px rgba(200, 255, 0, 0.18), 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
4. **Active & Hover Radiance:** When an interactive element gains state focus, it emits an ultra-sharp, non-blurry inner refraction line (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(13, 242, 201, 0.4)`) paired with a concentrated local glow (`0 0 24px -4px rgba(13, 242, 201, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft/Technical** curvature discipline (Level 1). Elements express sharp, surgical poise rather than bubbly, consumer-grade curves.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Sculpted with a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This gives components a diamond-cut, milled-metal edge.
- **Primary Panels & Ledger Cards:** Styled with `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px). Curves remain understated, maintaining strong architectural lines across multi-column grids.
- **Modals & Flyouts:** Maximize at `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) to subtly soften large floating panels without deviating into organic softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Execution (Prismatic Mint):** Solid fill of `#0DF2C9` with high-contrast obsidian typography (`#040608`), semi-bold weight. Borderless, but paired with a 1px pseudo-inset top highlight. Hover triggers an energetic shift toward `#C8FF00` (Solar Lime) and a concentrated cyan floor glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Atelier:** Surface of `#0F1522` with a full dichroic border gradient (`#0DF2C9` to `#7928CA` at 30% opacity). Text in pure `#F2F5F8`. Hover scales border opacity to 80% and applies a subtle micro-lift.
- **Destructive / Risk Trigger:** Obsidian backdrop framed with a razor border of `#FF2E63`, text colored in `#FF2E63`. Hover fills with `rgba(255, 46, 99, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- Built with monospaced `label-caps` typography. Height locked to 22px.
- Positive delta chips use dark obsidian backgrounds with `rgba(13, 242, 201, 0.12)` wash, razor-sharp 1px mint border, and `#0DF2C9` text.
- Alpha status tags leverage the Solar Lime `#C8FF00` palette to highlight market-beating strategies.

### Inputs & Terminal Fields
- Background set to deep obsidian well `#06080D` recessed beneath card tiering.
- Resting border is a subdued slate-graphite (`rgba(132, 146, 166, 0.2)`).
- On active focus, the border snaps to solid `#0DF2C9` with an outer optical aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(13, 242, 201, 0.2)`).
- Numerical input fields automatically enforce tabular alignment using JetBrains Mono with embedded asset tickers positioned as non-interactive caps badges.

### Ledger & Asset Cards
- Structured with modular headers: metadata tag at top-left, tabular asset price and delta top-right.
- Hairline horizontal divider lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Integrated real-time sparkline telemetry utilizes stroke-gradient fills transitioning dynamically from Prismatic Mint (`#0DF2C9`) on upward trends to Radiant Coral (`#FF2E63`) across market contractions.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkboxes are 16px geometric squares with a 2px bevel. Unchecked state is bordered in dark slate. Checked state triggers an instant solid `#0DF2C9` fill with an obsidian icon glyph.
- Segmented switches employ recessed track foundations (`#06080D`) with sliding thumb plates featuring dichroic edge sheens and crisp white typography.
